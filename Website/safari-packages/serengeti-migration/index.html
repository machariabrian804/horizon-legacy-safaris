Package Overview

About this safari

The Serengeti is roughly fourteen thousand square kilometres of grass and granite, and the migration moves across it through the year. Six days gives you time to follow the herds rather than chase them: river crossings in the north when the season is right, calving grounds in the south-east when it is not, and lion, cheetah and leopard working the same country throughout.

Best for: Wildlife travellers who can be flexible on dates and want the herds.

About Serengeti National Park

Derived from the Maasai word 'Siringet' meaning 'endless plains', Serengeti National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site spanning nearly 15,000 square kilometers of golden grassland in northern Tanzania. It forms the southern portion of the greater Serengeti-Mara ecosystem. The Serengeti hosts the Great Migration year-round, following an ancient cyclical rhythm from the southern calving grounds in Ndutu through the central Seronera predator territory and across the crocodile-filled Grumeti and Mara rivers. With over 3,000 resident lions, the Serengeti boasts the largest lion population in Africa.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

A working outline. Every day can be re-paced, extended or swapped for a lodge you prefer.

  1. Day 1

    Arusha to the central Serengeti

    Collect in Arusha, drive via the Ngorongoro highlands and into the park, with a game drive en route to camp at Seronera. Evening drive.

  2. Day 2

    Seronera

    Full day in the central Serengeti: the river lines, the kopjes and the big-cat territories around the Seronera valley.

  3. Day 3

    Central to the north or south

    Move with the herds, north toward the Mara river if the season is right, or south toward the short-grass plains when they are not.

  4. Day 4

    North or south

    Full day in whichever sector the herds are using, with a picnic lunch and an unhurried pace.

  5. Day 5

    Return toward the central plains

    A last morning drive in the herd sector, then south toward the Naabi gate area.

  6. Day 6

    Serengeti to Arusha

    Early game drive out of the park, then the drive back to Arusha, arriving in the evening.
