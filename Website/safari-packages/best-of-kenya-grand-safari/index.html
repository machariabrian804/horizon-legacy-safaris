Package Overview

About this safari

Ten days is where a Kenya trip stops being a single safari and becomes a proper journey. This route runs north first, through Samburu and Laikipia, then down the Rift Valley to Naivasha and Nakuru, and finishes with three nights in the Mara. It is sequenced so each park adds a different landscape rather than repeating the last one.

Best for: Travellers making one long Kenya trip who want variety rather than one park.

About Samburu, Laikipia, Naivasha, Nakuru & the Mara

Maasai Mara National Reserve is globally celebrated as one of Africa's greatest wildlife reserves. Bordering Tanzania's Serengeti, the Mara encompasses over 1,500 square kilometers of pristine savannah, rolling hills, and acacia-dotted grasslands. It is home to an extraordinary concentration of big cats, including prides of lions, elusive leopards, and swift cheetahs. Between July and October, the reserve hosts the world-famous Great Wildebeest Migration, where over two million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles brave predator-infested Mara River crossings in search of lush pastures. Beyond the migration, the Mara offers breathtaking year-round safari game drives, sunrise hot air balloon safaris, and enriching cultural encounters with the traditional Maasai pastoralist communities.

Day by Day

Your Itinerary

A working outline. Every day can be re-paced, extended or swapped for a lodge you prefer.

  1. Day 1

    Nairobi to Samburu

    North past Mount Kenya to Samburu, arriving in the afternoon for a river drive.

  2. Day 2

    Full day in Samburu

    Dawn drive for the special five, afternoon on the river bends.

  3. Day 3

    Samburu to Laikipia

    South to a Laikipia conservancy, afternoon drive and a night drive.

  4. Day 4

    Laikipia

    Rhino monitoring, a guided walk and a full afternoon out on the conservancy.

  5. Day 5

    Laikipia to Naivasha

    Drive south into the Rift Valley for a boat trip and a walk on Crescent Island.

  6. Day 6

    Naivasha to Nakuru

    Morning at leisure, then into Lake Nakuru National Park for an afternoon drive.

  7. Day 7

    Nakuru to the Mara

    Drive across the Rift and into the Mara for a late-afternoon game drive.

  8. Day 8

    Full day in the Mara

    Dawn-to-dusk game viewing with a picnic lunch on the plains.

  9. Day 9

    Second Mara day

    River-line drives and big-cat tracking, with a relaxed middle of the day.

  10. Day 10

    Morning drive and return

    Final early drive, breakfast, then the road or flight back to Nairobi.
