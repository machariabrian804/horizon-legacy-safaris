Sample Safari Itineraries

7-Day Best of Kenya Safari

A seven-day route that links the elephants of Amboseli with the Rift Valley lakes and finishes in the Maasai Mara. It is the shape we use most often when a first Kenya trip has one week: one southern park, two lakes, and three Mara nights for the big cats.

Duration: 7 days / 6 nights

Amboseli National Park→ Lake Naivasha→ Lake Nakuru National Park→ Maasai Mara National Reserve
  1. Day 1 Amboseli National Park

    Arrival and first look at Amboseli

    Arrive in Amboseli and settle in, then take a late-afternoon drive into the park as the light drops and the elephant herds move toward the swamps.

    Stay: Amboseli area - camp or lodge matched to your budget band

    Transfer: Road transfer from Nairobi into Amboseli

  2. Day 2 Amboseli National Park

    A full day with the elephants

    A dawn drive when Kilimanjaro is usually clearest, then the swamps and Observation Hill through the middle of the day. Time with the big-tusker herds is the point of the day, with the mountain behind them.

    Stay: Amboseli area

    Transfer: Road transfer from Amboseli to Lake Naivasha

  3. Day 3 Lake Naivasha

    North to the Rift Valley water

    Travel from Amboseli to Lake Naivasha, watching the country change as the road climbs into the Rift Valley. The afternoon is left open for optional lake experiences: a boat trip past hippo and fish eagle, or a walk on Crescent Island.

    Stay: Naivasha lakeside

    Transfer: Road transfer from Naivasha to Lake Nakuru

  4. Day 4 Lake Nakuru National Park

    Rhino and flamingo country

    Travel to Lake Nakuru National Park for a day of wildlife viewing: black and white rhino, Rothschild giraffe, buffalo and flamingo along the alkaline shore. Baboon Cliff and the northern shoreline give the widest views over the lake.

    Stay: Nakuru area

    Transfer: Road transfer from Nakuru into the Maasai Mara

  5. Day 5 Maasai Mara National Reserve

    Into the Mara

    Travel to the Maasai Mara and settle in. An afternoon game drive follows, subject to arrival time, working the nearest river line as elephant and buffalo come down to drink.

    Stay: Mara area - camp or lodge on the reserve or a bordering conservancy

    Transfer: Full day in the reserve, no transfer

  6. Day 6 Maasai Mara National Reserve

    A full day in the Mara

    A full-day safari with opportunities for the Big Five and everything else the reserve holds: lion, cheetah, elephant, buffalo and, with patience, leopard. Picnic lunch on the plains and a late return to camp.

    Stay: Mara area

    Transfer: Final morning in the Mara, then departure

  7. Day 7 Maasai Mara National Reserve

    Final morning and departure

    One last early game drive or a slow breakfast overlooking the plains, followed by departure arrangements: the road back to Nairobi, or a light-aircraft flight from a Mara airstrip.

    Stay: Departure day

Please note

  • This is an illustrative itinerary, not a confirmed departure. Dates, lodges and the order of the middle days are set at quotation.
  • Wildlife sightings - including elephants, rhino and the Big Five - are never guaranteed.
  • Accommodation is suggested by area and budget band only. Property names are shared with your written quote.
  • Any of the seven days can be extended, re-paced or swapped. Ask us to rebuild the route around your dates.
