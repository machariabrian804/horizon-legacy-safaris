Package Overview

About this safari

A short, high-impact safari for the festive holiday window. Amboseli's swamps draw some of Africa's last great tuskers, and the snow-capped summit of Kilimanjaro frames almost every game drive. Festive departures fill quickly, so we plan road and air logistics around the dates you hold.

Best for: Families and couples travelling over the festive break with three days to spare.

Trip Highlights

Big-tusker elephant herds in the Enkongo Narok and Longinye swamps
Kilimanjaro views at first light and again at sunset
Observation Hill panoramas across the dried Lake Amboseli bed
Festive-period game drives paced for families and mixed-age groups
Optional Maasai community visit arranged with local hosts
Day by Day

Your Itinerary

A working outline. Every day can be re-paced, extended or swapped for a lodge you prefer.

  1. Day 1

    Nairobi to Amboseli

    Depart Nairobi after breakfast and drive south through the Kapiti plains, dropping into the Amboseli basin by early afternoon. Settle in, then take a late-afternoon game drive toward the swamps as elephants move in to drink.

  2. Day 2

    Full day in the park

    A dawn game drive when Kilimanjaro is usually clearest, back for breakfast, then out again through the middle of the day to Observation Hill and the Longinye swamp circuit. Sundowners on the plains before returning to camp.

  3. Day 3

    Morning drive and return

    Final early game drive, breakfast at camp, then the drive back to Nairobi with a picnic stop en route, arriving late afternoon.
