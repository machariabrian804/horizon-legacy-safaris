DAY 01

Scenic Rift Valley Journey, Check-in & Poolside Sunset

08:00 AM
Departure from Nairobi & Great Rift Valley Escarpment Viewpoint

Depart Nairobi in the morning. Make a refreshing stop at the majestic Rift Valley viewpoint for jaw-dropping photos overlooking Mt. Longonot and the valley floor.

12:30 PM
Arrival & VIP Welcome at The Encore Hotel Nakuru

Arrive in Nakuru. Receive a refreshing welcome mocktail and towel, proceed with swift check-in, and settle into your air-conditioned, luxury room.

01:30 PM
Exquisite Buffet Lunch

Delight in a sumptuous 3-course buffet lunch at the hotel’s main restaurant, featuring both local Kenyan barbecue and international specialties.

03:30 PM
Afternoon Leisure: Heated Swimming Pool or Optional Menengai Crater Excursion

Spend a leisurely afternoon dipping into the crystal-clear heated pool, sunbathing, or take a short excursion to Menengai Crater or Hyrax Hill museum.

07:30 PM
Gourmet Dinner & Evening Lounge Drinks

Savor an atmospheric dinner under subtle mood lighting. Unwind with good music at the lounge bar or relax by the garden firepit under star-lit skies.

DAY 02

Lake Nakuru Wildlife Safari & Leisurely Return

📍 Nakuru → Nairobi 🍽️ Gourmet Breakfast & Lunch 🦏 Rhino & Bird Sanctuary
06:30 AM
Optional Early Morning Lake Nakuru Safari Game Drive

Rise early for an exhilarating game drive at Lake Nakuru National Park. Spot endangered white and black rhinos, Rothschild giraffes, lions, zebras, and millions of flamingos feeding along the saline shores.

09:00 AM
Lavish Champagne & Farm-Fresh Breakfast

Return to the hotel for a hearty breakfast feast: made-to-order omelets, fresh tropical fruits, warm pastries, fresh juices, and aromatic Kenyan tea & coffee.

11:00 AM
Relaxed Morning & Souvenir Shopping

Take one final dip in the pool, stroll through the manicured grounds, capture keepsake photographs, and browse local artisan crafts nearby.

12:30 PM
Check-out & Farewell Lunch

Check out with fond memories. Enjoy your included departure lunch before embarking on a relaxed drive back to Nairobi, arriving by late afternoon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, payments, hotel rules, and travel logistics.

Booking is simple and fast! Click any "Book Now" or "WhatsApp Us" button. You can reserve your room with a 30% commitment deposit via official M-PESA Till / Paybill or Bank Transfer. The remaining 70% balance is payable 3 days before your check-in date. Once deposit is made, you will receive an official booking voucher and receipt.

Standard hotel check-in time is 12:00 PM (Noon) and check-out is 10:00 AM. If you arrive early, you are welcome to enjoy the pool, lounge, and lunch while your room is finalized. Late check-out can be requested upon booking and is granted based on room availability.

Yes! We provide convenient roundtrip transport from Nairobi in a modern, customized 4x4 Safari Land Cruiser or Safari Tour Van with a pop-up roof for game viewing. Pick-up and drop-off are available from your Nairobi residence, hotel, or JKIA airport. Select the "With Transport" option in our pricing table or notify us on WhatsApp.

We recommend comfortable lightweight casual clothing for daytime, swimwear for the heated pool, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a camera/binoculars for safari viewing. Nakuru evenings can be comfortably cool, so carry a light fleece jacket or cardigan. Don't forget your national ID or passport for park admission and hotel registration!

We understand plans can change! You may reschedule your booking free of charge up to 7 days before your departure date, subject to room availability. Cancellations made more than 10 days in advance receive an 85% refund or 100% travel credit valid for 12 months.
