Destination Overview

Introduction to Egypt - Land of the Pharaohs

Egypt is the cradle of human civilization, where five thousand years of monumental history line the life-giving waters of the River Nile. From the towering Great Pyramids of Giza and the enigmatic Sphinx to the colossal temples of Luxor and Karnak, Egypt is an unparalleled journey through antiquity.

A luxury cruise along the Nile between Luxor and Aswan carries travelers past palm-lined riverbanks, timeless mudbrick villages, and rock-cut pharaonic tombs. In the Valley of the Kings, the underground burial chambers of Tutankhamun and Ramesses gleam with radiant hieroglyphic colors preserved for millennia.
